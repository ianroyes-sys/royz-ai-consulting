## Title

## Description

Give one coffee SKU three distinct visual directions. I create AI-assisted, human-reviewed static ad concepts using your approved product assets and exact copy.

The package includes three concepts, each exported in square (1080×1080) and portrait (1080×1350): six PNGs total, with one consolidated revision round. I check packaging, wording, layout and export size against your approved brief.

Please provide one SKU/product page, high-resolution product photos, logo, brand colors/fonts, approved copy and claims, visual examples, and confirmation that you may use the supplied assets. Contact me on Fiverr before ordering so we can confirm fit and scope.

This is a static creative package. Video, additional SKUs, media buying, editable source files and new directions after approval are separate. No advertising performance or revenue guarantee. Portfolio examples are fictional spec concepts, not client campaign results.

## FAQ

**Will this increase sales?** No result is guaranteed. The files provide distinct creative directions for your team to test.

**What is one revision?** One consolidated list of changes within the approved concept direction. New products, additional concepts or a different direction need a new scope.
